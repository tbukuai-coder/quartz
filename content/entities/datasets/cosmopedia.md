# Cosmopedia

> Hugging Face's **synthetic textbook dataset** — generated by [[entities/models/mistral|Mixtral 8x7B]] across hundreds of topics, used as a key pretraining component for [[entities/models/smollm|SmolLM2]].

## Overview
~30B tokens of textbook-like content generated via topic × audience matrix prompting. Validates that synthetic data is viable for pretraining, not just fine-tuning.

## Method
1. **Seed topics**: Curated list across STEM, humanities, arts
2. **Audience variation**: Children, students, experts
3. **Style variation**: Textbook, tutorial, explainer
4. **Generation**: Mixtral with structured templates
5. **Filtering**: Remove low-quality, repetitive content

## Usage
- [[entities/models/smollm|SmolLM2]]: Key pretraining component alongside FineWeb-Edu, FineMath
- Part of the synthetic data trend ([[sources/phi-4|Phi-4]], [[sources/magpie|Magpie]])

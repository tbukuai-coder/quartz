# Shanghai AI Lab / OpenGVLab

> A leading Chinese AI research institution behind the InternVL series — the most downloaded open-source vision-language model family on HF Hub.

## Overview
Shanghai AI Lab (also known as Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory) is a Chinese national research institution focused on fundamental AI research. Through its OpenGVLab group, it has produced the InternVL series, which has become the most prominent open-source alternative to GPT-4V and GPT-4o in multimodal AI.

## Key Contributions
- **InternVL series**: InternVL 1.0 → [[sources/internvl-1-5|1.5]] → 2.0 → [[sources/internvl-2-5|2.5]] — continuously improving open VLMs
- **InternViT-6B**: A 6B-parameter vision encoder that can be continuously trained and reused across different LLMs
- **First open MLLM >70% on MMMU** (InternVL 2.5)
- **InternLM series**: Language models used as backbones for InternVL

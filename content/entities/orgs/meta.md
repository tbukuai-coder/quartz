# Meta AI

> Creators of the **LLaMA/Llama** family — the single most impactful decision in open-source AI was Meta's release of LLaMA weights, catalyzing the entire community.

## Key Contributions
- [[entities/models/llama|LLaMA/Llama family]] — 7B to 405B parameters, progressively more open
- Proved that public data alone produces competitive models
- Released detailed RLHF documentation (Llama 2)
- Open-sourced core infrastructure (PyTorch, fairseq, etc.)

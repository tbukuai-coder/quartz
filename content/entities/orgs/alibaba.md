# Alibaba / Qwen Team

> Developers of the **Qwen** model series — the most comprehensive open LLM family with sizes from 0.6B to 235B, supporting 119 languages, with unified thinking/non-thinking modes for reasoning. Also producing leading vision-language models (Qwen2.5-VL). Qwen3 is the most upvoted model paper on HF Papers (339 upvotes).

## Key Contributions
- [[entities/models/qwen|Qwen3 series]] — state-of-the-art open reasoning models with unified thinking modes
- [[entities/models/qwen|Qwen2.5 series]] — widest size range of any open model family, 18T token pretraining corpus
- [[sources/qwen25-vl|Qwen2.5-VL]] — vision-language models matching GPT-4o on document understanding, with visual agent capabilities
- **Qwen3-30B-A3B** matches DeepSeek-R1 (671B) on many reasoning benchmarks — with 3B active params
- **119 languages** in Qwen3 (up from 29 in Qwen2.5)
- Specialized variants: Qwen2.5-Coder, Qwen2.5-Math, Qwen2.5-Omni
- All Qwen3 models released under Apache 2.0

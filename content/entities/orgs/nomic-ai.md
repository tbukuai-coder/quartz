# Nomic AI

> Developers of **Nomic Embed** — the first fully open-source, open-data embedding model to beat OpenAI's closed alternatives. Champions of full reproducibility in ML: open weights, open training code, and open training data.

## Key Contributions
- **nomic-embed-text-v1** — First fully open embedding model to outperform OpenAI Ada-002
- **nomic-bert-2048** — Modified BERT with RoPE and SwiGLU for long-context encoding
- 235M curated contrastive training pairs released openly
- **Nomic Atlas** — data visualization and exploration platform
- **GPT4All** — ecosystem for running LLMs locally

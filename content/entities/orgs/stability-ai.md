# Stability AI

> The company behind **Stable Diffusion**, the most widely used open-source image generation model — catalyzing an explosion in open generative art, community fine-tunes, and consumer-accessible AI creativity tools.

## Overview
Stability AI funded and released Stable Diffusion in 2022, making high-quality text-to-image generation available to everyone. Based on the [[sources/latent-diffusion|Latent Diffusion Model]] architecture, Stable Diffusion moved image generation from expensive cloud APIs to consumer GPUs, spawning a massive ecosystem.

## Key Contributions
- [[entities/models/stable-diffusion|Stable Diffusion 1.5]] (2022) — First widely adopted open image generation model
- **SD 2.0/2.1** (2022) — OpenCLIP, v-prediction, 768px
- [[sources/sdxl|SDXL]] (2023) — Dominant open text-to-image model (2M+ downloads, 27K ⭐)
- **Stable Diffusion 3** (2024) — DiT architecture, MMDiT, flow matching
- **Stable Video Diffusion** — Open video generation
- **Stable Audio** — Audio generation models

## Ecosystem Impact

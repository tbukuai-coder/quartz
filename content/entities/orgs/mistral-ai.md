# Mistral AI

> French AI company founded by ex-Meta and ex-DeepMind researchers — known for **efficient models** that outperform much larger competitors and pioneering open-source **Sparse MoE**.

## Key Contributions
- [[entities/models/mistral|Mistral 7B]] — 7B model outperforming Llama 2 13B
- [[entities/models/mistral|Mixtral 8x7B]] — First open Sparse MoE, matching Llama 2 70B
- Sliding Window Attention and Grouped-Query Attention popularization
- Apache 2.0 licensing for core models

# Anthropic

> Introduced **Constitutional AI** and **RLAIF** — showing that AI systems can be aligned using written principles and self-critique rather than extensive human annotation.

## Key Contributions
- **Constitutional AI** — alignment via AI self-critique against written principles ([[sources/constitutional-ai]])
- **RLAIF** — RL from AI Feedback (AI generates preference labels instead of humans)
- **Claude** series of models
- Advanced the field of AI safety research

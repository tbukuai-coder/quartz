# Cohere / Cohere For AI

> A Canadian AI company building enterprise LLMs and the non-profit research lab **Cohere For AI** — creators of the [[sources/aya|Aya]] multilingual model family (101 languages, 3,000+ contributors), Command-R (RAG-optimized models), Aya Vision (multilingual multimodal), and leading advocates for **multilingual AI equity**.

## Overview
Cohere operates on two fronts: (1) commercial enterprise LLMs (Command series) optimized for RAG and tool use, and (2) open research through Cohere For AI, focusing on multilingual inclusion. Their Aya project is the largest community-driven multilingual AI effort, involving 3,000+ contributors from 119 countries.

## Key Contributions

### Open Research (Cohere For AI)
- **[[sources/aya|Aya-101]]** (2024): Instruction-tuned model covering 101 languages (50%+ lower-resourced)
- **Aya-23** (2024): Focused 23-language model (8B/35B), strong quality
- **Aya-Expanse** (2024): SOTA multilingual at 8B and 32B scales
- **[[sources/aya-vision|Aya Vision]]** (2025): Multilingual multimodal model with synthetic annotation framework and cross-modal model merging — 8B rivals Llama-3.2-90B-Vision
- **Aya Dataset**: 204K human-curated instruction pairs across 65 languages
- **Aya Collection**: 513M instances across 114 languages

### Commercial Models
- **Command-R** (2024): 35B RAG-optimized model with tool use and citation
- **Command-R+** (2024): 104B enhanced version
- **Embed v3**: Multilingual embedding model for 100+ languages
- **Rerank v3**: Cross-encoder reranker for search/RAG

## Significance
- Largest community effort for multilingual AI (3K contributors, 119 countries)
- Command-R pioneered the RAG-first model design (grounded generation with citations)
- Aya Vision extends multilingual mission to the multimodal frontier with best-in-class performance
- Embed/Rerank v3 widely used in enterprise RAG pipelines
- Advocates for serving languages beyond the top 10–20

# Google / Google DeepMind

> Creators of the **Transformer** architecture, **BERT**, and the **Gemma** open model family — foundational contributions that underpin the entire field.

## Key Contributions
- **Transformer architecture** — the foundation of all modern LLMs ([[sources/attention-is-all-you-need]])
- **BERT** — established the pre-train + fine-tune paradigm ([[sources/bert]])
- **Gemma** — open models from Gemini research ([[sources/gemma]])
- **AI Co-Mathematician** — interactive agentic workbench for mathematicians, 48% FrontierMath Tier 4 SOTA ([[sources/ai-co-mathematician]])
- T5, PaLM, Gemini (proprietary frontier models)
- FLAN — instruction tuning methodology

# Moonshot AI

> Chinese AI company behind the **Kimi** series of models. Demonstrated that **simple RL scaling** (without MCTS or process reward models) can achieve o1-level reasoning, introducing the concept of **context length as a new scaling axis** for reasoning.

## Key Contributions
- **Kimi k1.5** — Multimodal LLM with o1-level reasoning via scaled RL
- Established that long-context RL (128K tokens) is a key ingredient for reasoning
- **Long2Short transfer** methods — distilling long-CoT into efficient short-CoT models
- Kimi Chat — one of China's leading consumer AI assistants

# T5: Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer

> Unified **all NLP tasks** into a single **text-to-text** format — every task (translation, summarization, classification, QA) is framed as "input text → output text." T5 established the **encoder-decoder** architecture standard and the **C4 dataset** (Colossal Clean Crawled Corpus). T5 models remain among the most downloaded on HF Hub.

## Key Contributions
- **Text-to-text framework**: Every NLP task → "translate English to German: ...", "summarize: ...", "classify: ..." — unified input/output format
- **Systematic study**: Compared pre-training objectives, architectures, data, and scale — the most thorough ablation of the transformer era
- **C4 dataset**: 750GB cleaned Common Crawl — became a standard pretraining corpus
- **Encoder-decoder architecture**: Validated that encoder-decoder outperforms decoder-only for many tasks (pre-GPT-3 era)
- **T5-11B**: Largest model at release, SOTA on multiple benchmarks

## Impact
- **Flan-T5** ([[sources/flan]]): Most downloaded encoder-decoder models on HF Hub
- **C4 dataset**: Used in countless pretraining experiments, including [[sources/dolma|Dolma]]
- **Text-to-text paradigm**: Influenced instruction-tuning formats (though decoder-only won for generation)
- **mT5**: Multilingual variant — base for [[sources/aya|Aya-101]]
- **UL2**: Google's follow-up unifying different pre-training objectives

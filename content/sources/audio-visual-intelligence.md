# Audio-Visual Intelligence in Large Foundation Models

> Comprehensive survey of Audio-Visual Intelligence (AVI), a multidisciplinary field integrating auditory and visual modalities through large foundation models for understanding, generation, and interaction.

## Key Contributions
- Introduces unified taxonomies and methodological foundations for Audio-Visual Intelligence (AVI)
- Covers tasks from understanding and generation to interaction with audio-visual modalities
- Surveys modality tokenization, cross-modal fusion, autoregressive and diffusion-based generation approaches
- Addresses large-scale pretraining, instruction alignment, and preference optimization for AVI
- Includes speech recognition, sound localization, audio-driven video synthesis, video-to-audio, dialogue, embodied interfaces, and agentic interfaces

## Method
The paper provides a comprehensive survey covering:
1. **Modality tokenization**: How audio and visual signals are tokenized for joint modeling
2. **Cross-modal fusion**: Techniques for combining audio and visual representations
3. **Generation paradigms**: Both autoregressive and diffusion-based approaches for audio-visual generation
4. **Pretraining and alignment**: Large-scale pretraining strategies and instruction alignment
5. **Downstream tasks**: Speech recognition, sound localization, audio-driven video synthesis, video-to-audio generation, dialogue systems
6. **Safety and controllability**: Synchronization, spatial reasoning, controllability, and safety considerations

## Results
- Comprehensive taxonomy and methodological foundations for the AVI field
- Integration of recent advances including Meta MovieGen and similar audio-visual foundation models
